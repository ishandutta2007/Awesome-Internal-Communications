# Awesome Internal Communications Platforms

**Internal Communications Platforms** (also called employee intranet or employee experience platforms) help organizations publish news, target messages, engage employees, share knowledge, and reach both desk-based and frontline workers across apps, email, and digital channels. Leading commercial platforms include Staffbase, Firstup, Workvivo, Simpplr, Interact, Axero, Haiilo, Beekeeper, Unily, Jostle, Poppulo, and ContactMonkey.

Below is a **curated list** of notable platforms and their open-source equivalents. While polished, multi-channel enterprise IC platforms are mostly commercial, a solid set of open-source intranet, social collaboration, and messaging tools can be combined to cover most internal communication needs.

## 🔓 Open-Source Software

### Open-Source Intranet & Employee Experience Platforms
- **[HumHub](https://www.humhub.com/)** — Flexible open-source social network and intranet framework. Supports spaces, activity streams, profiles, modules, and is highly extensible. Popular base for modern internal communities.
- **[eXo Platform](https://www.exoplatform.com/)** — Open-source digital workplace with social intranet features, wikis, documents, tasks, spaces, and collaboration tools. Available as community edition.
- **[Open Intranet](https://www.open-intranet.com/)** (Drupal-based) — Modern open-source intranet distribution focused on news, knowledge, documents, people, and employee engagement with full data ownership and no per-user fees.
- **[Liferay Community Edition](https://www.liferay.com/)** — Mature Java-based digital experience platform widely used for enterprise intranets, portals, and content-driven internal sites.
- **Plone**, **Drupal**, and **WordPress** (with intranet-oriented distributions or plugins) — Flexible open-source CMS foundations that many organizations use to build custom internal communication hubs.

### Team Messaging & Collaboration Foundations
- **[Mattermost](https://mattermost.com/)**, **[Rocket.Chat](https://www.rocket.chat/)**, and **[Zulip](https://zulip.com/)** — Leading open-source team messaging platforms that can serve as the real-time communication layer of an internal communications stack. Fully self-hostable with strong security and compliance options.

### Supporting Building Blocks
- Open-source wiki and knowledge-base tools (e.g., Wiki.js, BookStack, Outline) for structured internal content.
- Newsletter and email campaign tools that can be self-hosted for targeted internal messaging.
- Digital signage and mobile-friendly frontends that integrate with the platforms above.

### Typical Open-Source Approach
1. **Intranet / news hub** — HumHub, eXo Platform, Open Intranet, or Liferay
2. **Real-time chat** — Mattermost, Rocket.Chat, or Zulip
3. **Knowledge base** — Dedicated open-source wiki or CMS modules
4. **Email / campaigns** — Self-hosted or integrated email tools
5. **Mobile & frontline reach** — Progressive web apps or mobile clients of the above platforms

This combination gives organizations full control over employee data, eliminates per-user IC licensing costs, and allows deep customization of branding, workflows, and integrations.
